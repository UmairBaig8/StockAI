## Problem Summary
- ₹100K paper capital → ₹100,121 (+0.12%) after entire day
- 180 positions opened, 160 closed, 352 trade events
- After 10:01 UTC: zero-profit death loop (buy/sell at identical prices)
- Optimizer crashes on startup (AttributeError: dict has no agent_provider)
- 126 loss-triggered postmortems on sub-0.1% moves (noise)

## Root Causes
1. BB oversold BUY → 2 min later BB overbought SELL at same price (oscillator loop)
2. Rust engine: when position not in HashMap, computes (signal.price - signal.price) = 0% P&L
3. Router passes dict instead of Settings object to OptimizerAgent
4. No minimum hold time, no price delta guard, no volume filter
5. signal_cooldown=120s too short, rsi_oversold=55 too high
6. Orchestrator hardcodes strategy_intent for postmortems
7. No daily loss limit, no circuit breaker
8. Postmortem fires on every -0.01% tick (noise flood)
9. Scheduler duplicate P&L report trigger (30s apart)
