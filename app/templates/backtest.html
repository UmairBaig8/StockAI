What is Backtesting?

Backtesting replays your trading strategy against historical price data to see how it would have performed — without risking real money.

It buys when RSI drops below oversold thresholds, sells on take-profit or stop-loss, and tracks every trade. Metrics like Sharpe ratio (risk-adjusted return) and max drawdown (worst peak-to-trough loss) help you evaluate the strategy objectively.

Pick tickers, period, and interval — then click Run.
